Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the privacy practices of the mobile applications and games published by Veoglobe (referred to as "we", "our", or "the developer") on the Google Play Store. As an independent, personal developer, we value your privacy and aim to be transparent about how your data is handled.

1. Offline Applications (No Internet Access)

Many of our applications and games are designed to run entirely offline. For these specific applications:

  • They do not require internet permissions.
  • They do not collect, transmit, or store any personal or technical data.
  • Any game progress or settings are saved locally on your device and are deleted if you uninstall the app.

2. Online Applications and Analytical Data

Some of our applications may require internet access to function or to help us understand usage patterns. These apps may use third-party services that collect data to analyze app performance.

Firebase Analytics (Google LLC)

We may use Firebase Analytics in selected apps to monitor performance, crash logs, and general user engagement. The data collected is aggregated and anonymous. It may include:

  • Device information (e.g., device model, operating system version).
  • App usage statistics (e.g., session duration, screens visited, active users).
  • Crash data to help us identify and fix software bugs.

We do not collect personally identifiable information (such as names, email addresses, or physical locations) through these analytics tools. You can view Google’s privacy practices here: Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Permissions

Our apps will only request permissions that are strictly necessary for core functionality. Any permission requested (such as storage or network access) will be explained in context within the app or on the Google Play Store listing.
