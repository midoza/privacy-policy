Veoglobe

Software Development & Electronic Exploration

Our Vision

Veoglobe is a personal development project focused on bridging the gap between hardware and software. The goal is to create lightweight, efficient mobile applications and games while exploring the integration of electronic boards to solve practical problems.

With a particular interest in sustainability, our research aims to develop applications and embedded systems that contribute to energy conservation, reducing unnecessary digital and electrical waste.

Development Pillars

Entertainment

Apps & Mobile Games

Developing engaging and user-friendly mobile applications. This includes interactive utility tools and lightweight games designed to run efficiently on a wide range of devices.

Hardware

Electronic Boards & Energy Saving

Designing and programming microcontrollers to interact with physical environments. Our focus is on low-power configurations to promote energy-efficient automation and hobbyist electronics.

Infrastructure

Multimedia APIs

Researching and building foundational API frameworks to handle media transport, metadata handling, and localized processing to support standalone applications.
